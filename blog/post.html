Building a calm commission sheet

March 12, 2026 · Mikaelleon

A commission form should feel like a desk, not a checkout maze. The sheet opens with type cards — bust, half, full — so price and scope sit in the same glance. A sticky cart keeps the total visible while you browse.

Step two holds guidelines, payment, and agreements. Separating selection from commitment reduces accidental submits and gives space for the legal quiet most studios need.

This demo stores the cart locally and issues a placeholder order ID. No backend yet — just enough structure to prove the flow before wiring mail or a queue admin.

If you are reading this from the archive, treat it as a studio note: keep the chrome sharp, the gold restrained, and the copy short enough to finish in one sitting.
